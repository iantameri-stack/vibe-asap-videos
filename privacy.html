Privacy Policy

Vibe Asap Videos

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

This application is a personal video editing tool. It accesses your TikTok account solely to upload videos you create.

Data Collection

No personal data is collected, stored, or shared with third parties. The app only uses TikTok's video upload functionality on your behalf.

TikTok Integration

This app uses the TikTok API to upload videos to your account. We only request the minimum permissions needed to publish videos. We do not access your followers, messages, or other personal information.

Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any third-party servers.
